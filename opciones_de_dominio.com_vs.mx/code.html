gavel Legal Considerations (FAQ)

Why use .mx or .com.mx?

For legal practices operating within Mexican jurisdiction, these extensions signal local compliance and expertise, significantly improving search rankings on google.com.mx.

Is .mx more expensive?

Yes, .mx is a premium localized extension. It often carries a higher registry fee due to its exclusivity and demand within the Mexican territory.

Can I have both .com and .mx?

Highly recommended. Owning both prevents competitors from squatting on your brand and allows you to redirect global traffic to your local Mexican landing page.
